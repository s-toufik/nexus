## How to use the nexus
1. **Before working on a project**, read its note and its latest journal entry: you know where we stopped.
2. **To understand something**, open its concept note and do the *Check yourself* questions.
3. **To master a domain**, follow a path from [[Paths]] and tick the steps.
4. **New idea or question?** Drop it in `80 Inbox`; it gets sorted into a concept or a project.

## How it grows
After each work session, the journal gets an entry, the project note its new state, new decisions their reasons, and new concepts their own note.
