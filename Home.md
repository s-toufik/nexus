## How to use it
1. **Before working on a project**, read its note and its latest log entry.
2. **To understand something**, open its note in Knowledge and answer *Check yourself*.
3. **To master a domain**, follow a path and tick its steps.
4. **Found something to read?** Add a note in Sources with why it's worth it.

## How it grows
After each work session: a log entry, the project note's new state, new decisions with their reasons, a note for each new idea.
