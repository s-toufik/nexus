# Nexus

> Everything we build, why it's built that way, and the ideas behind it.

## Where things are
- [[Projects]] — what we build, and why.
- [[Knowledge]] — what things are and how they work, in your words.
- [[Paths]] — in what order to learn them.
- [[Sources]] — what others wrote, and the reading list.
- [[Log]] — what happened, and when.
- [[Conventions]] — how the nexus is kept: where a note goes.

## In progress
- [[Homelab]] — the home server on Kubernetes.

## How to use it
1. **Before working on a project**, read its note and its latest log entry.
2. **To understand something**, open its note in Knowledge and answer *Check yourself*.
3. **To master a domain**, follow a path and tick its steps.
4. **Found something to read?** Add a note in Sources with why it's worth it.

## How it grows
After each work session: a log entry, the project note's new state, new decisions with their reasons, a note for each new idea.
